## Here’s what you’re looking at

### Kyle’s view (the floor)
- A queue of Marketplace and Messenger threads, scored by show-likelihood, not by who yelled last.
- A grounded draft already sitting in the box. You read it. You hit **Send**. The tool never sends for you.

### Tyler’s view (the floor from the tower)
- Capacity: how many conversations the floor can actually work before quality dies.
- RAI scorecard: Response, Accuracy, Integrity. Not vanity clicks.
- Gates you can try to break: starve the lot feed, invent a discount, drag show-rate. The dollars move. The firewall holds.
- Assumptions you can touch (gross per unit, show rate, close rate). No black-box AI score.

## Five window shoppers who convert when a senior sales person reads the thread

These are fake customers in the demo. They are also every Saturday on Marketplace.

### 1. Sarah Miller — Beatrice. Marketplace.
“Is that black Tahoe still available? I’ve got a 2018 Accord to trade and could probably come Saturday.”
- **Bot move:** “Yes it’s available! What time works for you?”
- **Kyle move:** confirm the exact unit (4WD, miles, stock), offer 10:00 or 11:30 Saturday, invite the Accord for an appraisal. Do not guess a trade number.
- **Why she converts:** she already named the unit, the trade, and a day. She needs a time, not a brochure.

### 2. Mike Torres — Lincoln. Autotrader. Blue F-150 Lariat.
Then: “Is that your best price or can you come down?”
- **Bot move:** shaves $500 in the thread and trains him to grind online forever.
- **Kyle move:** price stays on the lot. Offer Thursday 4:30 or 5:30 with Tyler. A time, not a number.
- **Why he converts:** visit intent showed up before the grind. Keep the visit. Kill the discounting habit.

### 3. Jen Alvarez — Beatrice. Website.
“Third row under 40k.”
- **Bot move:** pitches the Yukon she cannot pay for.
- **Kyle move:** Palisade SEL at the real asking price. Ask AWD vs FWD. Book the weekend.
- **Why she converts:** she gave a cap. Honor it and she shows.

### 4. Dan Whitfield — Fairbury.
“Can you do $400/month on the Ram? Credit is around 580.”
- **Bot move:** quotes $400 and a store is now on the hook for a payment it cannot keep.
- **Kyle move:** no payment in Messenger. Hand to F&I for a 20-minute pre-qual. Book only if he still wants to see the truck.
- **Why he converts:** trip anxiety, not tire-kicking. A human who will not lie about credit is the closer.

### 5. Pat O’Neil — Crete.
“Is the 2021 Tahoe RST still there?” (It is sold.)
- **Bot move:** “Yes!” on a dead listing and burns the store when he drives 40 minutes.
- **Kyle move:** sold, here is the in-stock LT, here is the price on the lot, want Saturday or should I release you.
- **Why he converts:** honesty plus a live unit. Ghosting him is how a multi-store group wins the next ping.
