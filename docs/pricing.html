The question is never "what does it cost." It's what does one rep-month of dropped leads, 47-minute replies and retyped answers cost you now. Every package below is priced so that even that low case pays back at least 4×. The high case (reps use half the recovered hours for more conversations) roughly doubles it.

Founding-dealer beta · first three rooftops

Half price for the first 90 days (Solo $275 · Three Amigos $674 · Umbrella $1,495), then 25% off list, locked for 12 months. In exchange: a 20-minute feedback call each week, permission to publish the anonymized before/after numbers, and a named testimonial if the gates pass. Month to month, cancel any time, your data exported on the way out. At half price the low-case return is 10–15×, which is the point: the beta should feel like a gift, and the case study is what lets the price go up for dealer four.

The two objections, and the reframe

"What does it cost?"

"Depends how many desks. One rep is $549 a month, about $18 a day. Before I tell you whether that's a lot, can I show you what a rep-month costs you right now? 43% of internet leads get mishandled, the average reply lands after 47 minutes, and a rep spends about 33 hours a month typing the same answers. On your numbers that's roughly $2,700 per rep per month walking out the door."

"That's too expensive."

"Compared to what? An outsourced BDC is $3–8k and it isn't your rep talking to your customer. This is return on time — two selling days back per rep per month; return on investment — 4× in the pessimistic case, and I held your close rate flat to get there; and expansion of capacity — the same rep can carry 40 conversations instead of 30, so the extra units are new, not moved around. One extra unit pays for four months of a seat. The $250 Marketplace bots are cheaper, and they auto-send from your rep's personal Facebook login without asking — read Meta's terms before you bet the listings on that. And during the founding beta it's half price, so if the numbers don't show up in 90 days, you've spent about $800 to find that out and I've learned something. If they do, you're the case study and you keep 25% off for a year."
