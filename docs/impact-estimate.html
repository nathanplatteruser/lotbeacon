LotBeacon — before & after, one rep, one month

Zoellner Ford pilot estimate · Sept 2026 · deliberately pessimistic
Every assumption listed below · try the interactive demo

An average rep spends roughly a third of the selling month posting to Facebook, answering DMs, retyping the same answers, and checking whether they said the right thing. Meanwhile 43% of internet leads get mishandled and the typical reply lands after 47 minutes, when the customer has already heard from someone else. LotBeacon doesn't make the rep a better closer. It makes sure every lead gets a fast, verified, on-tone reply and a booked slot, so the rep's month converts more of what marketing already paid for.

Assumptions we chose to be conservative about

Held flat or ignored: close rate on the showroom floor (41%); Marketplace posting time; any lift from the six reply styles; any lift from the momentum tracker or buddy notes; service, referral and repeat-buyer effects. Rounded against us: assisted reply time 2.5 min (demo measures 1.5–3.0); set rate 48% (phone leads hit 75%); show rate +2 pts only. Volume: the high case assumes reps use about half the recovered hours to carry 40 conversations instead of 30, still well under the ~3× capacity the reply-time math allows. Gross: $2,400 per used unit, front + back, editable in the app's Owner dashboard. Store size: 6 reps, ~150 internet/Messenger leads per month (NADA average), 30 per rep.
